Lip art for Lip Sync Generator — the spec the built-in styles follow
===================================================================

THE FILES

  One PNG per shape, nine shapes:   X A B C D E F G H
  PNG-24 with a real alpha channel. No background, no matte, no white card.
  1024 x 1024, square.
  Name each file with an isolated letter: D.png, mouth-D.png and
  04_D_wide.png all land on D.

  1024 is the size the app will ever ask for: a mouth is drawn at up to
  1024px across, so anything bigger is weight with nothing to show for it
  and anything smaller gets upscaled at the extremes. Draw at 2048 if that
  suits you and downsample once on the way out.

  SVG works too and is better if your tool exports clean paths — it is a
  fraction of the weight and sharp at any size.

THE FRAME — the part that matters

  The app scales the WHOLE file into the mouth box and centres it. It never
  trims. So the framing has to be baked into the files.

  1. ALL NINE SHARE ONE FRAME. Draw them on one canvas, in register, and
     export each shape on that same canvas. Do not trim each shape to its own
     bounds: a closed mouth trimmed tight would balloon to the width of a
     wide-open one, and the mouth would pulse as it plays.

  2. THE SET IS CENTRED, NOT EACH SHAPE. Put the nine together — the union of
     all the ink — in the middle of the tile. An individual shape sits wherever
     it sits inside that.

  3. SIZE ON THE TILE. Measured across the twenty-one built-in styles, the
     nine together span:

         width   0.45 to 0.65 of the tile   (median 0.51)   = about 520px
         height  0.31 to 0.49 of the tile   (median 0.42)   = about 430px
         centre  0.500, 0.501               dead centre

     Aim at 520 x 430 centred in the 1024 tile and the set will sit at the
     same scale as Cartoon and Line art — switch styles mid-clip and the
     mouth will not jump.

  4. WHAT SETS THOSE NUMBERS. C (open) is the widest shape in most styles and
     D (wide open) is the tallest in every one of them. Draw those two first:
     they fix the frame, and the other seven are placed inside it.

THE NINE

  X  rest, closed, neutral          E  rounded — "oh"
  A  M B P — lips pressed together  F  puckered — "oo", "w"
  B  consonants, barely open        G  teeth on lip — "f", "v"
  C  open — "eh"                    H  tongue — "l", "th"
  D  wide open — "ah"

  The set is never allowed to draw a blank frame: a missing shape falls back
  to A, then X. The chip shows a badge like 7/9 so a half-finished set is
  visible at a glance.

WHERE THEY GO

  As your own set:   a folder inside lips/ beside the served HTML, or the
                     folder picker under Custom lips in the Mouth tab.
  In the standard set: the built-ins are drawn in code, so pictures would be
                     embedded in the HTML itself. Send the nine and that is a
                     half-hour job.

  lip-tile-template.png in this folder is a 1024 tile with the target box
  marked. Put it under your drawing as a guide layer and delete it before
  exporting.
